About me

Pianist, producer
& educator.

Making music theory and technique genuinely understandable, without dumbing it down. Here's how I got here.

Book a Lesson Read the tips

My journey into music began at just seven years old when I picked up the viola and joined my local orchestra. It wasn't long before my curiosity led me to the keyboard and guitar, where I spent hours teaching myself intricate finger-style pieces by ear, often tackling music far beyond my level. But it was at 11, when I discovered the piano, that everything changed. The instrument's expressive range and limitless possibilities captivated me, setting me on a path that would define my career.

Though my practice had given me an exceptional ear, I struggled with reading music, a challenge that might have deterred others but instead fuelled my obsession with mastering theory and technique. By 13 I was invited to study GCSE Music alongside students three years my senior, and by 16 I had achieved Grade 8 ABRSM Piano, bypassing every previous grade. My passion for teaching emerged early too, mentoring students from the age of 15 while leading choirs and composing for school ensembles.

That dedication earned me first place in multiple piano competitions, including a Beethoven Sonata Competition and a 20th-Century Music Competition. Determined to push further, I achieved my ATCL Piano Performance Diploma with distinction and secured my place at the world-renowned Royal Northern College of Music, studying alongside some of the world's most able pianists.

As a performer, my career has spanned solo recitals, festivals, big-band performances and collaborations with celebrated instrumentalists and vocalists, always chasing depth of expression alongside technical detail.

Beyond performance, education has always been at the heart of what I do. At just 18 I founded my own music school, mentoring hundreds of students and leading a team of instructors. In 2021 I moved from a physical school to sharing what I know on a global scale. Today I'm committed to helping musicians of all levels unlock their creative potential, because passion and enjoyment are essential to real musical mastery.
