About Matthew

From viola at seven to teaching thousands online.

Matthew discovered the piano at 11 and never looked back, achieving Grade 8 ABRSM by 16 and his ATCL Performance Diploma with distinction. An exceptional ear paired with a hard-won mastery of theory shapes how he teaches today: clear, practical, and rooted in why the music works.

With 143K YouTube subscribers and millions of views, he's built a reputation for making the hard things make sense.
